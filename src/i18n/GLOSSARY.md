# Glossary

Fixed translations of the terms TermiPaw uses. Use them everywhere, including in backend messages, so the same thing is never called two names. Security warnings should be plain rather than polite.

English and Simplified Chinese are written and reviewed by the maintainer. The other languages were translated with AI and have not been reviewed by native speakers; corrections are welcome.
